Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Inova Painting Corp (“Inova”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and when you contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collectHow we use itCalls & textsSharingCookiesYour choicesContact

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a quote or contact us by form, phone, text or email, we collect the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, the service you need and project details.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, pages viewed and the date and time of your visit. We use it only to keep the website secure and working.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and schedule an in-person assessment.
  • To prepare and send estimates and to perform the work you hire us for.
  • To communicate with you about your project by phone, text or email.
  • To protect the website against spam, fraud and abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Phone calls and text messages

If you give us your phone number, you agree that we may call or text you about your request and your project. Message frequency depends on your project; message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to stop text messages at any time. We do not sell or share your mobile number with third parties for their marketing.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run this website and our business, and only as needed:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers quote requests from this website to our email.
  • Our marketing partner’s lead-management system, which records your quote request together with how you found us (for example the ad campaign or search that brought you), so we can follow up quickly and measure our advertising.
  • Hostinger, which hosts this website.
  • Content providers that load parts of this page in your browser: Google Fonts (typography), Esri / OpenStreetMap (service-area map) and the public CDNs cdnjs, jsDelivr and unpkg (website code). These providers may receive your IP address as part of delivering their content.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, or as part of a sale or transfer of our business.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies by itself. When you arrive from an online ad or link, we keep the campaign details from that link (such as UTM parameters or ad click IDs) in your browser’s session storage until you close the tab, and send them with your quote request. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future (for example Google Analytics, Google Ads or Meta Pixel) to measure our ads and improve the website, they may use cookies or similar technologies, and we will update this policy. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

6. Third-party links

Our website links to other sites, such as Thumbtack, Instagram and Facebook. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

7. Data retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to you, complete your project, keep business records and meet legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

8. Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by using the contact details below. Depending on the state where you live, you may have additional privacy rights; we will honor them as required by law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last changed.
